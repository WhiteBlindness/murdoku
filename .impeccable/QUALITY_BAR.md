# Quality Bar: Continuity Desk

The shipped world is a working 1940s film-editing continuity desk, not a decorative noir dashboard.

- **Frame:** boxed case masthead, bone continuity strip, dominant reconstructed floorplan, overlapping right-side dossier, one dark transport rail.
- **Depth:** real graphite and archival-paper raster material, physical paper edges, restrained directional lift, no hard zero-blur drops.
- **Lettering:** condensed industrial headlines, typewritten evidence, narrow technical labels.
- **Ornament density:** film perforations, clipped/torn paper edges, contact-sheet frames, magnetic step numbers, grease-pencil amber only where evidence is active.
- **Motion:** one authored projector-path reveal connects the selected literal clue to its board target; placement snaps like a magnetic marker; reduced motion remains calm.
- **Ceiling test:** the first solving viewport must be remembered as “the evidence strip projecting onto the reconstruction board,” while room names, furniture, controls, and accusation remain immediately legible.
